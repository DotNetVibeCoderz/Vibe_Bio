# Biotech.Net

## Ultimate Biotechnology & Bioinformatics Platform for .NET

> Visi: Menjadi kombinasi Bioconductor + Biopython untuk platform .NET.

# Executive Summary

Biotech.Net adalah platform open-source Biotechnology, Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, Genomics, Proteomics, Drug Discovery, Synthetic Biology, dan Biotech AI terbesar untuk ekosistem .NET.

## Foundation

- GravicodeScience
- HF.Net
- RLNet
- Visual.Net

## Goals

- Native .NET experience
- Cross-platform
- Notebook-first
- AI-ready
- Rust accelerated
- Enterprise-ready
